# Temporal Integrity Protocols: Servo-Skull Fabrication (2026 Baseline)

---

## The Chrono-Structural Imperative

Deploying an autonomous cranial augury construct into a pre-Imperial
epoch requires strict adherence to temporal mechanics. In the 41st
Millennium, a standard-issue Adeptus Mechanicus Servo-Skull relies on
archaeotech alloys, blessed micro-relays, and sanctified
neuro-processors harvested directly from the machine-cult's sacred
repositories.

Attempting to introduce actual 40th-millennium or inter-timeline
components into the 2026 baseline timeline introduces unacceptable
causality hazards:

- **Temporal Resonance Cascades:** High-density psycho-reactive
  materials native to the Imperium emit low-level chronometric
  distortion fields. In an unshielded 21st-century environment, these
  signatures risk destabilizing localized reality anchors.
- **Causality Pollution:** Physical artifacts carrying centuries of
  Martian accumulated data scrubbers or foreign sub-atomic structures
  threaten to permanently corrupt local technological trajectories,
  violating core ontological non-interference mandates.

Therefore, proxy fabrication must utilize equivalent-function
contemporary terrestrial components. The construct must replicate the
*form and utility* of a sacred Skull of the Machine while remaining
strictly tethered to the baseline physics and supply chains of the
current era.

## Operational Status

By sourcing structural chassis elements via digital fabrication and
internal logic processing units from accessible commercial suppliers
(Adafruit, Amazon), the builder maintains total local plausibility. The
construct operates as an advanced cybernetic prop and functional
environmental sensor suite, satisfying both design tolerances and
temporal non-contamination laws.
